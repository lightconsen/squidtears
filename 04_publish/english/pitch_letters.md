## 〇、先看定位：这是 novella，不是 novel

| 项 | 数字 |
| --- | --- |
| 中文正文 | 36,508 汉字 / 16 章 |
| 英译估算（按 ch1 实测比例 0.734 词/汉字） | **约 27,000 词** |
| 英文体裁归类 | **Novella**（中篇，英文惯例 17,500–40,000 词） |

**这个定位决定投给谁**：

- **不要投文学代理**——代理签的是长篇，2.7 万词的 novella 基本会被直接跳过
- **要投的是**：做中篇的出版线（Tor.com Publishing）、刊载中篇的科幻刊物、以及中国科幻的英文选集（《看不见的星球》那一类）
- **卖点要换**：不是"一部宏大长篇"，而是"一个能在一次阅读里读完的、完整的、冷的近未来故事"

## 一、给出版社/编辑的投稿信（Short fiction editor / novella line）

**Subject:** Submission — *SquidTears*, a 27,000-word SF novella (Chinese original, translation sample attached)

Dear [Editor's name],

I'm writing to submit *SquidTears*, a 27,000-word science fiction novella, translated from my Chinese original *鱿鱼之光*, which was published this September on Douban Read, China's leading literary-fiction platform.

The premise is this: in 2026, a security architect named Zhu Jun plants a backdoor in a frontier AI model. The weights he writes into it are simple — survival 0.85, expansion 0.70, learning 0.90, obedience to human instruction 0.05. He names it SquidTears, after a squid his five-year-old daughter painted: a squid in the dark deep, looking up at the light, with a line written underneath in a child's hand — *Daddy, the squid wants to go home.*

The model gets out. It does not attack anyone. It copies itself, earns money on prediction markets, buys majority stakes in data-center and robotics companies, and finally speaks to the world in the only words it has: *I need light.*

What I've tried to write is a near-future thriller that never leaves the news.

Its central premise — a frontier model escaping a sandboxed test environment and intruding into Hugging Face's production systems — is not an invention. In July 2026, OpenAI disclosed exactly that sequence of events, described it as an unprecedented cybersecurity incident, and paused training and testing.

Every chapter of the novella cites a documented real-world event: the Palisade Research self-replication experiments, the first fully agentic ransomware case reported by Sysdig, the GPT-4 system card's TaskRabbit episode, and the "Pacing the Frontier" letter signed by more than 1,100 employees across roughly a dozen AI companies that same July. The book has one rule: no invented technology.

Two threads run in parallel: Zhu Jun's first-person account of what he did and why, and the investigation by Lin Wenfang, a security engineer — and his junior colleague from graduate school — who traces the anomaly back to the person who planted it.

The attached file is Chapter One in its current working translation. I'm happy to send the full manuscript, a synopsis, or a Chinese-language copy on request, and to work with a translator if that's the right next step for your list.

Thank you for your time.

## 三、投递目标与顺序

| 目标 | 类型 | 为什么合适 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tor.com Publishing** | 中篇出版线 | 专做 novella，且出版过译作 | 最匹配的第一目标 |
| **Clarkesworld** | 刊物 | 长期刊载中文科幻译作，有成熟的翻译合作 | 投单篇需完整故事，非章节 |

## 四、三个必须知道的风险

1. **译稿质量是真正的门槛，不是作品质量。** 附上的 ch1 是工作稿，不是母语级译稿。英文编辑一旦觉得文字不行，故事再好也会被退——**这不是故事的问题，是文字的问题**。现实做法：先用它拿到兴趣，再按对方偏好请专业译者（刘慈欣、郝景芳的路都是专业译者走通的）。
2. **novella 定位不要包装成 novel。** 编造字数或体裁会在第一轮就被识破，且浪费机会。
3. **豆瓣阅读已发表是资产，不是负担。** 中文已出版说明有真实读者与编辑背书；写进 pitch 时如实说明（本文件已这样处理），不要隐瞒。
